Podium Flashing

Tool to flash 83_IMAGE to the harman/kardon Invoke Speaker

  1. Choose a NAND image below, or pick your own 83_IMAGE file.
  2. Hold down the reset button.
  3. While holding reset, turn on the device.
  4. Press the mute button 4 times.
  5. Wait for the yellow light to appear, then release reset.
  6. Click Connect & flash and choose the device.
